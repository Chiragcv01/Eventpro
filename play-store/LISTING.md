## English (en-IN)

**App name** (26/30)
SabManage: Tent & Catering

**Short description** (76/80)
Bookings, stock, catering, GST bills and payments for tent houses & caterers

**Full description**

SabManage keeps the full hisaab of your tent house, catering or event business in one app.

Made for tent houses, caterers, halwais, decorators and event managers who run weddings, receptions, sangeet, jagrans and corporate events.

BOOKINGS AND CALENDAR
• Every enquiry, quotation and confirmed event in one diary
• Peak saaya and muhurat dates marked on the calendar
• Run-sheet for each function: sound check, baraat, dinner, phere

GODOWN STOCK
• Shamiana, chairs, sofas, lights, DG sets and utensils with date-wise availability
• Stock is held automatically for confirmed bookings, with a warning when something is short
• Truck loading list, delivery challan, return and damage charges

CATERING CALCULATOR
• Pick the menu and guest count to get the halwai's full samagri list
• Raw cost from your own mandi rates and a suggested per-plate price
• Save the menu to the booking

GST BILLS WITH YOUR LOGO
• Quotations, tax invoices, receipts and challans with your logo, stamp, GSTIN and UPI ID
• CGST/SGST or IGST worked out for you
• Send as PDF or on WhatsApp

PAYMENTS AND REMINDERS
• Record advances and balances
• See who owes what and send a polite WhatsApp reminder in one tap

CREW, VENDORS AND PROFIT
• Daily crew attendance by event, advances and monthly payout
• Vendor purchase orders and dues
• Real profit of every event after vendors, expenses and wages

YOUR TEAM, YOUR RULES
• Create logins for your manager, accountant, supervisor and crew
• Decide exactly which sections each person can see or change
• Optional 6-digit Authenticator code for extra login safety

CLIENT PAGE
• Share a link so your client sees setup progress, programme, bill and balance, and leaves a review

HINDI AND ENGLISH
• Switch the whole app between Hindi and English with one tap

Start with a free trial. Your data stays in the cloud and only your team can see it.

## App access (for Google's reviewers)

The app needs a login, so Play asks for one. Create a demo business from Super Admin → Add business (for example "Review Demo Tent House"), add a few bookings, and give its login ID and password under Policy → App content → App access. Keep this login active.
